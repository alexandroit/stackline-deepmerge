# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, appearance, disability,
experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, clear
technical disagreement, and attention to the needs of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, or publishing
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and community activity
that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical.
